## 1. SERVICES

1.1 The Contractor shall provide a Fleet & Port Performance Analytics platform, comprising:
   (a) ingestion of AIS vessel-tracking data, terminal operating system data from Khalifa Bin Salman Port, and the Client's booking and container-tracking systems;
   (b) port-turnaround, berth-utilisation and container dwell-time analytics;
   (c) freight demand forecasting by lane (GCC, Indian subcontinent and East Africa); and
   (d) executive and operations dashboards.
