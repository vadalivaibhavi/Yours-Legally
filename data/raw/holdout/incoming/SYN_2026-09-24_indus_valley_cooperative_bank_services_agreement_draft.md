## 1. BACKGROUND

1.1 Pursuant to its RFP No. IVCB/IT/2026/14 dated 2 September 2026, the Bank has selected the Service Provider to implement a **Credit Early Warning System (EWS)** for its MSME, gold-loan and retail loan portfolios, in line with RBI expectations on early identification of stressed assets.

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 The Service Provider shall:
   (a) build data pipelines from the Bank's core banking system (Finacle), loan origination system and credit bureau feeds;
   (b) develop early-warning signals and a delinquency-prediction model (30/60/90 DPD);
   (c) deliver EWS dashboards and SMA (Special Mention Account) reporting; and
   (d) provide twelve (12) months of model monitoring and recalibration.

2.2 The Service Provider's RFP response (Annexure B) forms part of this Agreement. It states: *"AtliQ has recently delivered a comparable EWS for a scheduled small finance bank in Mumbai, and will apply the same proven feature framework and modelling approach, reducing time to value to 16 weeks."*
