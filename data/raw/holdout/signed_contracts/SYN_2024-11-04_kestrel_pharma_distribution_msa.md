# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is executed at Ahmedabad on this 4th day of November 2024 ("**Effective Date**")

**BY AND BETWEEN**

**KESTREL PHARMA DISTRIBUTION PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 7th Floor, Shivalik Corporate Park, Satellite Road, Ahmedabad 380015, Gujarat, India (hereinafter the "**Client**", which expression shall include its successors and permitted assigns) of the ONE PART;

**AND**

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India (hereinafter the "**Service Provider**", which expression shall include its successors and permitted assigns) of the OTHER PART.

## RECITALS

WHEREAS the Client is engaged in the wholesale distribution of pharmaceutical and over-the-counter healthcare products to approximately 6,200 chemists, hospitals and sub-stockists across nine states of India;

WHEREAS the Client wishes to implement demand forecasting, inventory optimisation and secondary-sales analytics across its depots; and

WHEREAS the Service Provider has represented that it has the expertise to provide such services.

NOW THEREFORE THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, in relation to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or the power to direct management.

1.2 "**Competing Business**" means any person or entity, other than the Client and its Affiliates, engaged (wholly or in part) in the wholesale distribution, stockist operations, carrying-and-forwarding or third-party logistics of pharmaceutical products in India.

1.3 "**Restricted Services**" means demand forecasting, inventory or replenishment optimisation, secondary-sales analytics and depot performance analytics, and any substantially similar data analytics services.

1.4 "**Deliverables**", "**Fees**", "**Milestone**", "**Milestone Fee**" and "**SOW**" have the meanings given in the SOW attached as Annexure A.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 The Service Provider shall provide the services described in each SOW ("**Services**"). SOW-1 covers a demand forecasting engine for 2,400 SKUs, depot-level replenishment recommendations, and Power BI dashboards for secondary sales.

2.2 Changes in scope shall be effected only through a written change request signed by both Parties.

## 3. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

3.1 The Client shall provide timely access to its ERP (SAP S/4HANA), depot management system and sales data, and nominate Mr. Hitesh Parikh as single point of contact.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The total Fees under SOW-1 are **USD 62,000**, payable against Milestones as specified in Annexure A.

4.2 The Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt. Fees are exclusive of GST. TDS may be deducted as required by law.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for three (3) years from the Effective Date, i.e. until 3 November 2027, unless terminated earlier.

5.2 Either Party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

5.3 The Client may terminate an SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice, subject to payment for Services rendered up to termination.

## 6. ACCEPTANCE

6.1 The Client shall test each Deliverable within ten (10) business days of delivery; absent written notice of material non-conformity, it shall be deemed accepted.

## 7. DELAY AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

7.1 For delay in achieving a Milestone solely attributable to the Service Provider, liquidated damages shall be 0.5% of the Milestone Fee per complete week of delay, not exceeding 5% of that Milestone Fee.

7.2 No liquidated damages shall be payable for delays caused by the Client, including delayed data access, or by Force Majeure.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each Party shall keep the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 On full payment, intellectual property in Deliverables created specifically for the Client shall vest in the Client.

9.2 The Service Provider retains its pre-existing tools, models, code libraries and know-how, and grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use those incorporated in the Deliverables for its internal business.

## 10. INDEMNITY

10.1 Each Party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its breach of confidentiality, infringement of third-party IP, or gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect or consequential losses, including loss of profit.

11.2 Each Party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable under the SOW giving rise to the claim, save for liability under Clause 12.

## 12. NON-COMPETITION

12.1 In consideration of the Client sharing its depot-level sales, margin and supplier data, the Service Provider undertakes that, during the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months after the expiry or termination of this Agreement (the "**Restricted Period**"), neither the Service Provider nor any of its Affiliates shall, directly or indirectly, provide any Restricted Services to any Competing Business.

12.2 The restriction in Clause 12.1 applies irrespective of whether the Competing Business is contracted directly or through an intermediary, reseller, partner or subcontractor, and irrespective of whether any Confidential Information of the Client is used.

12.3 The Client may waive Clause 12.1 in respect of a specific engagement only by prior written consent signed by a director of the Client, which may be granted or withheld at the Client's sole discretion.

12.4 The Parties acknowledge that the restriction is reasonable and necessary to protect the Client's legitimate business interests. Breach of this Clause shall entitle the Client to injunctive relief and to recover from the Service Provider all Fees paid under this Agreement as liquidated damages, without prejudice to other remedies.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION

13.1 The Service Provider shall process personal data only for the purpose of the Services and in compliance with applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as and when in force.

## 14. NON-SOLICITATION

14.1 During the Term and for six (6) months thereafter, neither Party shall solicit employees of the other involved in the Services.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

15.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

15.2 Disputes not resolved amicably within thirty (30) days shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated at Ahmedabad. The language shall be English.

## 16. MISCELLANEOUS

16.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent.

16.2 This Agreement and its SOWs are the entire agreement between the Parties and may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.
