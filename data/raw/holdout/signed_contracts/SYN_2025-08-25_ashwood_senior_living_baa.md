# BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

This Business Associate Agreement ("**BAA**") is entered into as of August 25, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Ashwood Senior Living, Inc.**, a Tennessee corporation with offices at 1600 West End Avenue, Suite 1100, Nashville, Tennessee 37203 ("**Covered Entity**"), and **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Business Associate**").

## RECITALS

A. Covered Entity operates 34 assisted-living and memory-care communities in Tennessee, Kentucky and Georgia.

B. Covered Entity and Business Associate are parties to a Professional Services Agreement dated August 25, 2025 (the "**Services Agreement**") under which Business Associate provides data engineering and analytics services that may involve Protected Health Information.

C. The parties enter into this BAA to comply with HIPAA, the HITECH Act and their implementing regulations at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in HIPAA, including "Protected Health Information" or "**PHI**", "Limited Data Set", "Breach", "Security Incident" and "Subcontractor".

## 2. PERMITTED USES AND DISCLOSURES

2.1 Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the services under the Services Agreement, or as required by law.

2.2 Business Associate shall not de-identify PHI for any purpose other than performing the services, and shall not use de-identified data for its own purposes or for other clients.

## 3. SAFEGUARDS

3.1 Business Associate shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards that comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164.

3.2 Business Associate shall encrypt PHI at rest and in transit using industry-standard encryption.

## 4. SUBCONTRACTORS AND LOCATION OF PROCESSING

4.1 Business Associate shall not engage any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI without Covered Entity's prior written approval.

4.2 Business Associate shall ensure that each approved Subcontractor agrees in writing, before any access, to the same restrictions and conditions that apply to Business Associate under this BAA. **Affiliates of Business Associate are Subcontractors for this purpose.**

4.3 **Offshore restriction.** Business Associate shall not, and shall ensure that its Subcontractors and Affiliates do not, access, store, process, transmit or view PHI — **including any Limited Data Set** — from any location outside the United States, whether directly or through remote access, virtual desktop or screen-sharing.

4.4 Data that has been de-identified in accordance with the Safe Harbor method at 45 C.F.R. §164.514(b)(2) may be accessed outside the United States only with Covered Entity's prior written approval for each data set.

## 5. REPORTING

5.1 Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this BAA, any Security Incident, and any Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and in any event within **five (5) business days** of discovery.

5.2 Business Associate shall reimburse Covered Entity's reasonable costs of breach notification, credit monitoring and regulatory response to the extent caused by Business Associate or its Subcontractors.

## 6. INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS

6.1 Business Associate shall make PHI available for access, amendment and accounting of disclosures as required by 45 C.F.R. §§164.524, 164.526 and 164.528, within ten (10) business days of request.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 This BAA is coterminous with the Services Agreement (initial term two (2) years, ending August 24, 2027), and continues for as long as Business Associate or any Subcontractor holds PHI.

7.2 Covered Entity may terminate the Services Agreement immediately if Business Associate materially breaches this BAA and does not cure within ten (10) days.

7.3 On termination, Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI and certify destruction in writing.

## 8. INSURANCE

8.1 Business Associate shall maintain cyber liability insurance with limits of not less than **USD 3,000,000** per claim, naming Covered Entity as additional insured, and provide a certificate on request.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Business Associate shall indemnify Covered Entity against losses, fines and penalties arising from Business Associate's or its Subcontractors' breach of this BAA. This indemnity is not subject to any limitation of liability in the Services Agreement.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 This BAA is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee and applicable federal law.

10.2 In the event of conflict between this BAA and the Services Agreement, this BAA controls.

10.3 Any amendment, including any approval under Clause 4, must be in writing and signed by Covered Entity's Privacy Officer.
