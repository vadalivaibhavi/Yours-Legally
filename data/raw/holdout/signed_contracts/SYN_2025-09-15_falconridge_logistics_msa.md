# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Contract Ref:** FLR/PROC/IT/2025/088
**Date:** 15 September 2025

## BETWEEN

**FALCONRIDGE LOGISTICS W.L.L.**, a limited liability company registered in the State of Qatar (CR No. 98112), with its principal office at Tower 3, Floor 17, Lusail Marina District, Lusail, Qatar (the "**Client**"); and

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated in India (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456), with its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, India (the "**Supplier**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, including any parent, subsidiary or sister company wherever incorporated.

1.2 "**Logistics Analytics Services**" means fleet utilisation, route optimisation, warehouse performance, port-turnaround, freight demand forecasting and similar data analytics or data engineering services for freight, shipping, warehousing or logistics operations.

1.3 "**Protected Customer**" means each person listed in Schedule 4, and any of its Affiliates.

1.4 "**Territory**" means the member states of the Gulf Cooperation Council.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the services described in Schedule 1 (the "**Services**"), comprising: a fleet and warehouse data platform; GPS telematics ingestion for 640 vehicles; warehouse-management-system integration for six warehouses; and Power BI dashboards for operations and customer account management.

2.2 The Client will, for the purposes of the Services, give the Supplier access to its customer account data, including volumes, lanes, tariffs and service levels for its key shipping and freight customers.

## 3. FEES

3.1 The total Contract Price is **USD 112,000**, payable by milestone per Schedule 2.

3.2 Invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

## 4. TERM

4.1 This Agreement is effective from the date above for a term of two (2) years, ending 14 September 2027, unless terminated earlier or extended in writing.

## 5. DELAY PENALTIES

5.1 If the Supplier fails to achieve a Milestone by its due date, the Client may deduct a delay penalty of one percent (1%) of the Milestone fee for each week of delay.

5.2 The aggregate of delay penalties shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the total Contract Price.

5.3 No delay penalty is payable to the extent the delay is caused by the Client.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Supplier shall keep strictly confidential all Client information, including customer lists, tariffs and volumes, during the term and for five (5) years thereafter.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 Deliverables created specifically for the Client vest in the Client on payment. The Supplier retains its pre-existing materials and grants a perpetual non-exclusive licence to use them within the Deliverables.

## 8. LIABILITY

8.1 Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the Contract Price, except for breach of Clauses 6 or 14.

## 9–13. [Warranties; Data Protection (Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016); Insurance; Force Majeure; Termination — standard provisions, see executed PDF pages 6–9]

## 14. PROTECTION OF CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIPS

14.1 The Supplier acknowledges that, in performing the Services, it will receive commercially sensitive information about the Client's relationships with the Protected Customers.

14.2 Accordingly, during the term of this Agreement and for twenty-four (24) months after its expiry or termination, the Supplier shall not, and shall procure that its Affiliates shall not, directly or indirectly, provide Logistics Analytics Services to any Protected Customer anywhere in the Territory, whether as contractor, subcontractor, partner or adviser.

14.3 This restriction applies whether or not any Client information is used.

14.4 The Client may release the Supplier from Clause 14.2 in respect of a named Protected Customer only by written notice signed by its Chief Executive Officer.

14.5 The Supplier shall pay the Client, as liquidated damages for each breach of Clause 14.2, an amount equal to fifty percent (50%) of the Contract Price, without prejudice to injunctive relief.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

15.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Qatar Financial Centre.

15.2 Disputes shall be referred to the Qatar International Court and Dispute Resolution Centre (QICDRC).
