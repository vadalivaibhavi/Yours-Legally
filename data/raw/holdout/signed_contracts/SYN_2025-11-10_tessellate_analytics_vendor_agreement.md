# VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

**AtliQ Template:** ATQ-VEN-IN v3.0 (as negotiated)
**Agreement Date:** 10 November 2025

This Vendor Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is made between:

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**AtliQ**"); and

**TESSELLATE ANALYTICS LLP**, a limited liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 (LLPIN AAT-4471), having its office at Plot 22, Kavuri Hills Phase 2, Madhapur, Hyderabad 500081, Telangana, India ("**Vendor**").

## 1. SERVICES

1.1 The Vendor shall provide data engineering personnel to support AtliQ's client projects as requested by AtliQ through work orders ("**Work Orders**").

1.2 Each Work Order shall specify the project, roles, number of personnel, expected hours and duration.

1.3 Vendor personnel shall work under the day-to-day direction of AtliQ's project leads and comply with AtliQ's and its clients' security policies.

## 2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement commences on 10 November 2025 and continues for two (2) years, until 9 November 2027.

2.2 "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve-month period starting on 10 November. The first Contract Year ends on 9 November 2026.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 AtliQ shall pay the Vendor at the blended rate of **INR 2,400 per hour** (exclusive of GST) for hours approved on timesheets.

3.2 The Vendor shall invoice monthly. AtliQ shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## 4. CAPACITY RESERVATION AND MINIMUM COMMITMENT

4.1 In consideration of the Vendor reserving a dedicated bench of four (4) data engineers for AtliQ and declining other engagements for that capacity, AtliQ commits to purchase not less than **1,500 hours of Services in each Contract Year** (the "**Minimum Commitment**").

4.2 If the hours invoiced in a Contract Year are less than the Minimum Commitment, AtliQ shall pay the Vendor a shortfall fee equal to **sixty percent (60%) of the hourly rate multiplied by the number of shortfall hours** (the "**Shortfall Fee**"), invoiced within fifteen (15) days of the end of the Contract Year and payable within thirty (30) days of invoice.

4.3 Hours performed by third-party vendors or freelancers engaged by AtliQ for data engineering work that the Vendor was able and available to perform shall not reduce the Minimum Commitment.

4.4 The Minimum Commitment for the second Contract Year may be reviewed in good faith by 30 September 2026, failing which it continues unchanged.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All work product created by Vendor personnel under a Work Order vests in AtliQ (or its client, as AtliQ directs) on creation. The Vendor retains its pre-existing tools and grants AtliQ a perpetual licence to use any incorporated in the work product.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep the other's (and, for the Vendor, AtliQ's clients') confidential information confidential during the term and for three (3) years thereafter.

## 7. LIABILITY

7.1 Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for breach of confidentiality or fraud.

7.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 8. DELAY

8.1 Where Vendor personnel fail to deliver an assigned task by the agreed date for reasons attributable to the Vendor, AtliQ may withhold 0.5% of the related monthly invoice per week of delay, capped at 5% of that invoice.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either party may terminate for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice; provided that if AtliQ terminates for convenience during a Contract Year, the Shortfall Fee for that Contract Year shall be calculated pro rata to the date of termination and becomes payable immediately.

9.2 Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within fifteen (15) days of notice.

## 10. NON-SOLICITATION

10.1 During the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, AtliQ shall not hire or engage, directly or indirectly, any Vendor personnel who performed Services under this Agreement. If AtliQ does so, it shall pay the Vendor a placement fee equal to thirty percent (30%) of that person's first-year annual compensation.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

11.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent.
