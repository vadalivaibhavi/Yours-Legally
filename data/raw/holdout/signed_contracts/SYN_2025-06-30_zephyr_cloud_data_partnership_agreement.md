# STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.:** ZCD-PTR-IN-2025-044
**Effective Date:** 30 June 2025

## PARTIES

1. **ZEPHYR CLOUD DATA PTE. LTD.**, a company incorporated in Singapore (UEN 201934567K) with its registered office at 8 Marina View, #29-01 Asia Square Tower 1, Singapore 018960 ("**Zephyr**"); and

2. **ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated in India (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456) with its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, India ("**Partner**").

## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 Zephyr provides the Zephyr Lakehouse cloud data platform. Partner provides data engineering and analytics consulting. The parties wish to jointly market and deliver Zephyr-based solutions in the Territory.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party.

2.2 "**Competing Platform**" means any cloud data warehouse, lakehouse or data platform product that competes with Zephyr Lakehouse, including without limitation those listed in Schedule 1, as Zephyr may update on thirty (30) days' notice.

2.3 "**Territory**" means India, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand and Vietnam.

2.4 "**BFSI Opportunity**" means any opportunity known to Partner or its Affiliates to provide data platform, data engineering or analytics services to a bank, non-banking financial company, insurer or other financial-services institution in India with an expected contract value exceeding USD 50,000.

## 3. PARTNER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Partner shall maintain at least three (3) Zephyr-certified engineers at all times.

3.2 Partner shall register all Zephyr-related opportunities in the Zephyr Partner Portal within ten (10) business days of identification.

3.3 Partner shall participate in at least two co-marketing activities per year.

## 4. ZEPHYR OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Zephyr shall pay Partner a referral fee of fifteen percent (15%) of first-year subscription revenue for registered opportunities that close within twelve (12) months.

4.2 Zephyr shall provide Partner with demo environments, training credits and partner-tier pricing.

## 5. FEES

5.1 Referral fees are payable quarterly in arrears within forty-five (45) days of quarter end.

## 6. TERM AND RENEWAL

6.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years, ending 29 June 2028.

6.2 Thereafter it renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred and twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

6.3 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within forty-five (45) days. There is no right to terminate for convenience during the initial term.

## 7. EXCLUSIVITY

7.1 During the Term and for six (6) months thereafter, Partner shall not, and shall procure that its Affiliates shall not, enter into or perform any reseller, referral, implementation, alliance or partnership agreement with the provider of any Competing Platform in respect of the Territory.

7.2 Clause 7.1 does not prevent Partner from performing services on a Competing Platform where the end customer has independently selected that platform before Partner's involvement and Partner receives no fee, margin or incentive from the provider of the Competing Platform.

## 8. RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL

8.1 Before submitting any proposal for a BFSI Opportunity, Partner shall notify Zephyr in writing (with reasonable details of the opportunity) and offer Zephyr the right to be proposed as the data platform.

8.2 Zephyr shall have fifteen (15) business days from notification to accept. If Zephyr accepts, Partner shall propose Zephyr Lakehouse as the primary platform in good faith. If Zephyr declines or does not respond, Partner may proceed on any platform, subject to Clause 7.

8.3 Partner shall maintain a written log of BFSI Opportunities and notifications, available to Zephyr on request.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information confidential during the Term and for three (3) years thereafter.

## 10. LIABILITY

10.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's liability is capped at the greater of USD 100,000 and the referral fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months, except for breach of Clauses 7, 8 or 9.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre, seated in Singapore, before a sole arbitrator.
