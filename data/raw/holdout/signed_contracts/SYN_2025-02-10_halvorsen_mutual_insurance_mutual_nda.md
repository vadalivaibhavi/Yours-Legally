# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is effective as of February 10, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") between:

**Halvorsen Mutual Insurance Company**, a Connecticut mutual insurance company with its principal office at 280 Trumbull Street, Hartford, Connecticut 06103 ("**Halvorsen**"); and

**AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**AtliQ**").

## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 The parties wish to evaluate a potential engagement under which AtliQ would build a claims-triage and fraud-indicator analytics solution for Halvorsen's personal auto and homeowners lines (the "**Purpose**").

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by either party to the other in connection with the Purpose, including claims data, loss ratios, fraud rules, underwriting guidelines, pricing, source code and solution designs.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is public, already known to the recipient, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Each party shall use the other's Confidential Information only for the Purpose, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to employees and advisers with a need to know who are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

3.2 Each party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, after giving prompt notice where lawful.

## 4. TERM

4.1 This Agreement covers disclosures made during the twelve (12) months following the Effective Date.

4.2 Obligations under Section 3 survive for three (3) years after the last disclosure.

## 5. RETURN OF INFORMATION

5.1 On request, each party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information.

## 6. NO OBLIGATION

6.1 Neither party is obliged to enter into any further agreement.

## 7. NO WARRANTY

7.1 Confidential Information is provided "as is".

## 8. NON-USE AND STANDSTILL

8.1 In recognition of the sensitivity of Halvorsen's claims and fraud data, for a period of **eighteen (18) months from the Effective Date**, AtliQ shall not develop, market, offer or provide any claims-triage, claims-fraud detection or claims-severity analytics product or service to any property and casualty insurer licensed in the United States, other than Halvorsen.

8.2 This Section 8 applies only to AtliQ.

## 9. NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 For twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Connecticut.

10.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject matter and may be amended only in writing.

10.3 This Agreement may be executed electronically.
