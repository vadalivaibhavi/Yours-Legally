# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 3, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Meridale Retail Brands, Inc.**, an Ohio corporation with offices at 41 South High Street, Suite 2600, Columbus, Ohio 43215 ("**Meridale**"); and

**AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Vendor**").

## 1. SERVICES

1.1 Vendor shall provide data engineering, analytics and business intelligence services as described in one or more Statements of Work ("**SOWs**") executed under this Agreement. SOW-1 (Exhibit B) covers a merchandising and markdown analytics platform for Meridale's 112 apparel and home stores.

1.2 Vendor may perform Services using personnel located in the United States or India, provided Meridale customer data is accessed only through Meridale-managed virtual desktops.

## 2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years (until February 2, 2028), unless earlier terminated.

2.2 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of notice. Meridale may terminate any SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice, paying for Services performed through the termination date.

## 3. FEES

3.1 Fees for SOW-1 are USD 96,000, payable by milestone as set out in Exhibit B.

3.2 Time-and-materials work, change orders and future SOWs shall be charged at the rates in Exhibit A ("**Rate Card**").

3.3 Undisputed invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in confidence, use it only for this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers with a need to know. Obligations survive three (3) years after termination.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Upon payment, Meridale owns Deliverables created specifically for it. Vendor retains its pre-existing tools and grants Meridale a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use those incorporated in Deliverables for internal purposes.

## 6. PRICING COMMITMENTS

6.1 **Rate Card.** The Rate Card rates are fixed for the Term.

6.2 **Volume discount.** Where aggregate fees invoiced in a contract year exceed USD 150,000, a 4% rebate applies to the excess.

6.3 **Most Favored Customer.** Vendor represents, warrants and covenants that the rates, fees, discounts and other pricing terms offered to Meridale under this Agreement are, and throughout the Term shall remain, at least as favorable as the rates, fees, discounts and pricing terms offered or charged by Vendor **or any of its Affiliates** to any other customer, anywhere in the world, for substantially similar services in substantially similar volumes.

6.4 **Adjustment.** If Vendor or any Affiliate offers or charges any other customer more favorable pricing, Vendor shall (a) notify Meridale in writing within thirty (30) days, (b) extend such pricing to Meridale effective from the date it was first offered to such other customer, and (c) promptly refund or credit any amounts paid by Meridale in excess of such pricing.

6.5 **Audit.** Once per contract year, on fifteen (15) business days' notice, Meridale or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality may audit Vendor's records to the extent reasonably necessary to verify compliance with this Section 6. If an audit reveals non-compliance, Vendor shall bear the cost of the audit in addition to the remedies in Section 6.4.

6.6 For the purposes of this Section 6, "**Affiliate**" means any entity controlling, controlled by or under common control with Vendor, including AtliQ Technologies Private Limited (India).

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 Vendor warrants that Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner and that Deliverables will materially conform to the SOW for ninety (90) days after acceptance.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Each party shall defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent arising from its breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages or lost profits.

9.2 Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid or payable under the applicable SOW in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for indemnification obligations and amounts due under Section 6.4.

## 10. DELAY

10.1 Liquidated damages for Vendor-caused milestone delay: 0.5% of the affected milestone fee per week, capped at 5% of that milestone fee. No liquidated damages for delays caused by Meridale or force majeure.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio. The state and federal courts in Franklin County, Ohio have exclusive jurisdiction.

11.2 **Insurance.** Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability of $1,000,000 and professional/cyber liability of $1,000,000.

11.3 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

11.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, with its Exhibits and SOWs, is the entire agreement of the parties.
