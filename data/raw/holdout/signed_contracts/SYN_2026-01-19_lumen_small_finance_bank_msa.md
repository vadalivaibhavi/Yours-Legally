# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is executed at Mumbai on 19 January 2026 ("**Effective Date**")

**BETWEEN**

**LUMEN SMALL FINANCE BANK LIMITED**, a banking company licensed by the Reserve Bank of India, having its registered office at Lumen Towers, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai 400051, Maharashtra, India (the "**Bank**");

**AND**

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India (the "**Service Provider**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Lumen EWS Model**" means the credit early-warning and delinquency-prediction model, feature pipelines, scorecards and associated code and documentation developed under SOW-1.

1.2 "**Final Acceptance**" means the Bank's written acceptance of Milestone M4 under SOW-1.

1.3 "**Regulated Lender**" means any bank, small finance bank, cooperative bank, non-banking financial company, housing finance company or other lender regulated by the Reserve Bank of India or the National Housing Bank.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 Under SOW-1 the Service Provider shall: (a) build feature pipelines over the Bank's loan management, bureau and collections data; (b) develop the Lumen EWS Model for MSME and retail loan books; and (c) implement monitoring dashboards and regulatory reporting extracts.

## 3. FEES

3.1 The Fees under SOW-1 are **INR 1,17,00,000** (approx. **USD 140,000**), payable against Milestones.

3.2 Undisputed invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days.

## 4. TERM

4.1 This Agreement remains in force for two (2) years from the Effective Date (until 18 January 2028), and continues to apply to any SOW in progress.

## 5. OUTSOURCING AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

5.1 The Service Provider acknowledges that the Services constitute outsourcing of financial services under the RBI Master Direction on Outsourcing of Information Technology Services and agrees to comply with all requirements notified by the Bank.

5.2 The Bank, its auditors and the Reserve Bank of India shall have the right to inspect and audit the Service Provider's premises, systems and records relating to the Services on reasonable notice.

## 6. DATA LOCALISATION

6.1 All Bank data, including customer and loan data and any derived data sets, shall be stored and processed exclusively within India.

6.2 No Bank data shall be accessed from outside India, including by any Affiliate of the Service Provider, without the Bank's prior written approval.

## 7. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

7.1 For delay attributable to the Service Provider, liquidated damages of 0.5% of the Milestone Fee per week, capped at 5% of that Milestone Fee. No LDs for delay caused by the Bank.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information strictly confidential. Obligations in respect of customer data survive indefinitely; all other obligations survive five (5) years after termination.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 The Lumen EWS Model and all Deliverables vest in the Bank upon payment.

9.2 The Service Provider retains its pre-existing tools and general methodologies.

## 10. RESTRICTIONS

10.1 The Service Provider shall not use the Bank's data, the Lumen EWS Model or any part of it for any other client.

10.2 Without limiting Clause 10.1, for a period of **twenty-four (24) months from the date of Final Acceptance** (the "**Cooling-off Period**"), the Service Provider shall not, and shall ensure that its Affiliates shall not, design, develop, deploy, license or provide any credit early-warning, delinquency-prediction or similar credit-risk monitoring model or service to any Regulated Lender in India, whether or not derived from the Lumen EWS Model.

10.3 The Bank may waive Clause 10.2 in writing for a specific engagement. Any request shall be made at least thirty (30) days before the Service Provider submits a proposal to the relevant Regulated Lender.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable under the relevant SOW, except for breach of Clauses 6, 8 or 10, or fraud.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Mumbai.
