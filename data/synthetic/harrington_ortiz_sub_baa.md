# SUBCONTRACTOR BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

**Between AtliQ Inc ("Business Associate") and Daniel Ortiz ("Subcontractor") — Harrington Health Systems, Contract No. HHS-IT-2026-0193**

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalised terms have the meanings given in the Business Associate Agreement between Harrington Health Systems, Inc. and AtliQ Inc (Exhibit D to Contract No. HHS-IT-2026-0193) (the "Upstream BAA") and in 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This Agreement applies only to the Harrington Health Systems engagement under Contract No. HHS-IT-2026-0193. Subcontractor may create, receive, maintain or transmit Protected Health Information only as necessary to perform services for that engagement and only after Harrington's prior written approval of Subcontractor has been obtained.

## 3. Obligations of Subcontractor

3.1 Subcontractor shall comply with every restriction and condition that applies to Business Associate under the Upstream BAA, including the requirement that Protected Health Information is accessed only from within the United States and only in environments controlled or approved by Harrington.

3.2 Subcontractor shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards that comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, complete HIPAA training, and pass the background check required by the Upstream BAA before any access.

3.3 Subcontractor shall not de-identify, aggregate or use Protected Health Information for any purpose other than the services.

## 4. Reporting

4.1 Subcontractor shall report to Business Associate any Privacy Event within twelve (12) hours of discovery.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 This Agreement ends when the Upstream BAA ends. On termination Subcontractor shall return or destroy all Protected Health Information and certify destruction in writing within ten (10) days.

## 6. General

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
