# Annex 3 — Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) incl. Standard Contractual Clauses

**to the Services Agreement Contract No. RWA-2026-014 between Rheinwerk Analytics GmbH ("Controller") and AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd ("Processor")**

## 1. Subject Matter and Duration

1.1 The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller solely for the performance of the Services Agreement Contract No. RWA-2026-014. This Annex applies for the term of the Services Agreement and for as long as the Processor holds personal data of the Controller.

1.2 Categories of data subjects: employees and technicians of the Controller's customers. Categories of personal data: names, user IDs, technician identifiers and free-text maintenance log entries that may contain names.

## 2. Instructions

2.1 The Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country.

2.2 The Processor shall inform the Controller without undue delay if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR.

## 3. Confidentiality and Security

3.1 The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data are bound by confidentiality.

3.2 The Processor shall implement the technical and organisational measures set out in Appendix 1 (encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, logging, remote access to the Controller's EU environment only).

## 4. Sub-processors

4.1 The Processor shall not engage another processor without the Controller's prior specific written authorisation. Where authorised, the Processor shall impose the same data protection obligations on the sub-processor by written contract and remains fully liable to the Controller for the sub-processor's performance.

4.2 No sub-processors are authorised at the date of this Annex.

## 5. Transfers to India

5.1 Transfers of personal data to the Processor in India are governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module 2 (controller to processor), which are incorporated by reference. Clause 17 option 1 applies with the law of Germany; the courts of Munich have jurisdiction under Clause 18.

5.2 Personal data shall remain stored in the Controller's EU cloud environment; the Processor's personnel in India access it remotely only.

## 6. Personal Data Breach

6.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

## 7. Assistance, Audit, Deletion

7.1 The Processor shall assist the Controller with data subject requests and data protection impact assessments, taking into account the nature of processing.

7.2 The Processor shall make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for audits, including inspections, by the Controller or an auditor mandated by it, on reasonable notice and no more than once per year unless a breach has occurred.

7.3 On termination, the Processor shall delete all personal data and certify deletion within thirty (30) days, unless storage is required by law.

## 8. Liability

8.1 Liability under this Annex is governed by § 9 of the Services Agreement.
