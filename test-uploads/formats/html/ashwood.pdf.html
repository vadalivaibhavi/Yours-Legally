Predictive Fall-Risk and Staffing Analytics

1. Parties

This SOW is entered into on ____________ 2026 between Ashwood Senior Living, Inc., 1600 West End Avenue, Suite 1100, Nashville, Tennessee 37203 ("Ashwood") and AtliQ Inc, 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("AtliQ").

2. Background

Under SOW-1 and SOW-2 AtliQ built Ashwood's resident-care data warehouse (electronic health record, medication administration and incident data) and operational dashboards. Ashwood now wishes to predict resident fall risk and align night-shift staffing to predicted risk.

3. Scope

3.1 Fall-risk model using resident age, diagnoses, medication classes, prior incidents and mobility assessments.

3.2 Night-shift staffing recommendations per community.

3.3 Integration of risk scores into Ashwood's nurse-station dashboards.

6. Staffing and Delivery Model (added by Ashwood — J. Coleman, 22 Sep 2026)

6.1 To meet Ashwood's target of go-live before 31 January 2027, AtliQ will supplement its U.S. team with four (4) data scientists from AtliQ's Pune, India analytics team (AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd).
