MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of ______________, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Greystone Claims Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 100 Pearl Street, 12th Floor, Hartford, Connecticut 06103 ("Greystone"), and AtliQ Inc, a Delaware corporation with offices at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("Provider").

  1. BACKGROUND

1.1 Greystone is a third-party claims administrator handling personal auto and property claims on behalf of eleven U.S. property and casualty insurers.

1.2 Greystone wishes to engage Provider to build a claims-triage and fraud-indicator analytics platform for use in its own claims operations.

  1. SERVICES

2.1 SOW-1 covers: (a) ingestion of first-notice-of-loss, adjuster notes and payment data from Greystone's Guidewire ClaimCenter instance; (b) a claims-complexity and fraud-indicator scoring model; and (c) adjuster workload dashboards.

2.2 Provider may use personnel in India for development, provided that claimant personal information is accessed only via Greystone's U.S.-hosted virtual desktop environment and is not downloaded.

  1. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 SOW-1 fees: USD 86,000, payable by milestone (Exhibit A).

3.2 Payment within thirty (30) days of invoice.

  1. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 Two (2) year term. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach (30 days). Greystone may terminate an SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice, paying for work performed and non-cancellable commitments.

  1. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Mutual. Each party protects the other's Confidential Information with reasonable care; obligations survive three (3) years, and indefinitely for trade secrets and claimant personal information.

  1. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 Greystone owns Deliverables created specifically for it on payment. Provider retains its pre-existing tools and know-how, and grants Greystone a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use those incorporated in Deliverables for internal purposes.

  1. WARRANTIES

7.1 Professional and workmanlike services; Deliverables conform to the SOW for ninety (90) days after acceptance.

  1. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent caused by its breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

  1. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

9.2 Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid or payable under the applicable SOW in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for indemnification obligations and breach of Section 5.

  1. DELAY

10.1 Liquidated damages of 0.5% of the affected milestone fee per week of Provider-caused delay, capped at 5% of that milestone fee; not applicable to delays caused by Greystone or force majeure.

  1. DATA SECURITY

11.1 Provider shall comply with the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act Safeguards Rule as applicable to service providers and with Greystone's security schedule; notify security incidents within seventy-two (72) hours.

  1. GENERAL

12.1 Governing law. Laws of the State of Delaware. Exclusive venue: state and federal courts in Wilmington, Delaware.

12.2 Insurance. Provider shall maintain professional liability and cyber liability insurance of $1,000,000 each.

12.3 Non-solicitation. Mutual, twelve (12) months, employees engaged in the Services.

12.4 Assignment. Neither party may assign without consent, except to a successor by merger.
