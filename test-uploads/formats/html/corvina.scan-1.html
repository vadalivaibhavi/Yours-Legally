DOCUMENT 1 — STATEMENT OF WORK No. 2

Retail Media & Loyalty Analytics

SOW Reference: ATQ-COR-SOW-02

Under: Master Services Agreement between Corvina Foods GmbH and AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd dated 23 March 2026, including Annex 2 (Data Processing Agreement) (the "MSA")

1. Parties

Corvina Foods GmbH, Großer Burstah 31, 20457 Hamburg, Germany ("Corvina") and AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd, 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, India ("AtliQ").

2. Start Date

2.1 Services under this SOW shall commence on 12 October 2026.

3. Scope

3.1 Ingestion of retailer loyalty-panel data (pseudonymised household IDs, basket data) from three German retail partners.

3.2 Customer-segment and promotion-uplift models.

3.3 Activation of audience segments to Corvina's retail-media platforms via a reverse-ETL connector.

4. Delivery Team and Tools

4.1 AtliQ will deliver with a Pune team of four, supplemented by Kriti Data Labs Pvt Ltd (Bengaluru, India), AtliQ's subcontractor, for model development (two data scientists).

4.2 Audience activation will use SyncRiver (SyncRiver Inc, Austin, Texas, USA), a reverse-ETL SaaS tool that copies segment tables into SyncRiver's US-hosted processing environment before pushing them to retail-media platforms.
