QUANTA LAKE IMPLEMENTATION PARTNER AGREEMENT

This Implementation Partner Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of last signature below (the "Effective Date") between Quanta Lake Data, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 500 Howard Street, Suite 900, San Francisco, California 94105 ("Quanta Lake"), and AtliQ Inc, a Delaware corporation with offices at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("Partner").

1. APPOINTMENT

1.1 Quanta Lake appoints Partner as a non-exclusive Select-tier Implementation Partner for the Quanta Lake data platform in the Territory.

1.2 "Territory" means India, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia and Qatar.

1.3 Partner may refer opportunities, resell Quanta Lake subscriptions at partner-tier pricing, and deliver implementation services to Quanta Lake customers.

2. PARTNER BENEFITS

2.1 Referral fee: 20% of first-year net subscription revenue for registered opportunities.

2.2 Resale margin: 18% off list price.

2.3 Market development funds of up to USD 40,000 per year, subject to joint marketing plans.

2.4 Partner shall be listed in the Quanta Lake partner directory and included in Quanta Lake's Asia-Pacific and Middle East launch campaign in Q4 2026.

3. PARTNER COMMITMENTS

3.1 Partner shall achieve and maintain at least five (5) Quanta Lake-certified engineers within six (6) months.

3.2 Partner shall register opportunities in the Quanta Lake Partner Portal.

3.3 Platform focus. During the Term, Partner shall not, and shall procure that its Affiliates shall not, enter into any new reseller or implementation partnership with the providers of Zephyr Lakehouse, Polarbase Warehouse or StratoGrid Analytics Cloud in the Territory.

4. TERM

4.1 Initial term of two (2) years, renewing annually unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice.

4.2 Either party may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days' notice.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Mutual; three (3) years after termination.

6. TRADEMARKS

6.1 Each party grants the other a limited licence to use its marks in approved co-marketing materials.

7. LIABILITY

7.1 Neither party is liable for indirect damages. Each party's liability is capped at USD 250,000, except for breach of confidentiality or Section 3.3.

8. GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. Disputes shall be resolved in the state or federal courts in San Francisco County.
