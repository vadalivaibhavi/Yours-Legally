RECITALS

A. The Company is a carrying-and-forwarding agent and super-stockist for pharmaceutical manufacturers, operating 11 temperature-controlled depots across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka and supplying approximately 3,800 chemists and hospitals.

B. The Company wishes to engage the Vendor to build a demand forecasting and depot replenishment platform.

1. SCOPE

1.1 The Vendor shall deliver:

(a) a SKU-level demand forecasting engine (approx. 1,900 SKUs, weekly granularity, 13-week horizon);

(b) depot-level replenishment and expiry-risk recommendations;

(c) secondary-sales and chemist-level analytics dashboards in Power BI; and

(d) integration with the Company's Marg ERP and the manufacturers' DMS feeds.

1.2 The Vendor represents that it has prior experience delivering demand forecasting for pharmaceutical distribution in India, and may draw on such experience and pre-built components to accelerate delivery.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

2.1 Total fees: INR 65,00,000 (approx. USD 78,000), plus GST, payable per Schedule A.

2.2 The Company shall pay undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt.

3. TERM

3.1 This Agreement commences on signature and continues for two (2) years.

4. TIMELINES AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

4.1 Time is of the essence.

4.2 For any delay in achieving a Milestone, the Vendor shall pay liquidated damages of one percent (1%) of the total fees for each week of delay, subject to a maximum of fifteen percent (15%) of the total fees.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All Deliverables shall vest in the Company upon creation.

5.2 The Vendor grants the Company and its group companies a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, sub-licensable licence to use any pre-existing Vendor tools or components incorporated in the Deliverables.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Vendor shall keep confidential all information of the Company and its principals (manufacturers), during the term and for five (5) years thereafter.

7. INDEMNITY

7.1 The Vendor shall indemnify the Company against all losses arising out of the Services or any act or omission of the Vendor or its personnel.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
