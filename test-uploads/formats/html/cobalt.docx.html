This Professional Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of ______________, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Cobalt Outfitters, Inc., a Colorado corporation with offices at 1700 Lincoln Street, Suite 3200, Denver, Colorado 80203 ("Cobalt"), and AtliQ Inc, a Delaware corporation with offices at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("Consultant").

1. SERVICES

1.1 Consultant shall provide the services described in each Statement of Work ("SOW"). SOW-1 covers an omnichannel inventory visibility platform for Cobalt's 46 outdoor-gear stores and e-commerce channel, including pipelines from Shopify Plus and Cobalt's NetSuite ERP, and Power BI dashboards for allocation and replenishment.

1.2 Consultant may use personnel located in India, provided customer personal data is not stored outside the United States.

2. FEES

2.1 SOW-1 is fixed-fee: USD 58,000, payable by milestone (Exhibit B).

2.2 Change orders and future SOWs shall be charged at the rates in Exhibit A, which shall remain fixed for thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.

2.3 Invoices are payable net thirty (30) days.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 Three-year term. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach on thirty (30) days' notice. Cobalt may terminate an SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice and shall pay for work performed.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Mutual. Three years post-termination.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Cobalt owns SOW deliverables upon payment. Consultant retains pre-existing tools, licensed to Cobalt non-exclusively and perpetually for internal use.

6. WARRANTIES

6.1 Professional and workmanlike performance; conformity to the SOW for sixty (60) days after acceptance.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 Neither party liable for consequential damages. Each party's aggregate liability capped at fees paid or payable under the applicable SOW, except for confidentiality breaches and indemnity.

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Mutual indemnity for third-party claims arising from IP infringement, breach of confidentiality, or gross negligence/willful misconduct.

9. PRICING

9.1 Most Favored Customer. Consultant warrants that the rates in Exhibit A are no less favorable than those Consultant charges any other customer in the United States for comparable services. If Consultant offers lower rates to another U.S. customer during the Term, Consultant shall extend them to Cobalt.

10. DELAY

10.1 Liquidated damages of 0.5% of the affected milestone fee per week of Consultant-caused delay, capped at 5% of that milestone fee.

11. GENERAL

11.1 Governed by the laws of the State of Colorado. Venue: Denver County.

11.2 Consultant shall maintain professional liability insurance of $1,000,000.
