# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**FinServe Ref:** FSC/LGL/MNDA/2026/118 (Revision 2)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into on ____________ 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**FINSERVE CAPITAL PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 11th Floor, One BKC Tower, G Block, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai 400051, Maharashtra, India ("**FinServe**"); and

**ATLIQ TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456; GSTIN 27AAKCA4821F1Z3), having its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**AtliQ**"),

each a "**Party**" and collectively the "**Parties**". A Party disclosing Confidential Information is the "**Disclosing Party**" and a Party receiving it is the "**Receiving Party**".

## RECITALS

A. The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning the development of data analytics and reporting solutions for FinServe's lending and wealth businesses (the "**Proposed Transaction**").

B. In connection with the Proposed Transaction, each Party expects to disclose information to the other, and the Parties wish to protect that information on equal terms.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all information, in any form, disclosed or made available by or on behalf of either Party to the other Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the Effective Date, including customer and portfolio data, credit models, product plans, pricing, technology architecture, methodologies, software, business processes, and all analyses, compilations, notes or other documents prepared by the Receiving Party that contain or reflect such information.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's directors, officers, employees and professional advisers.

## 2. USE AND PROTECTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 Each Receiving Party shall:
   (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating and, if applicable, performing the Proposed Transaction;
   (b) keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information confidential and not disclose it to any person other than its Representatives who need to know it and are bound by obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
   (c) protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care; and
   (d) be responsible for any failure by its Representatives to comply with this Agreement.

2.2 Neither Party shall, without the other Party's prior written consent, disclose to any person the fact that discussions are taking place or any of their terms, except to its Representatives on a need-to-know basis.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

3.1 The obligations in Clause 2 shall not apply to information which the Receiving Party can demonstrate by written evidence:
   (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through an act or omission of the Receiving Party or its Representatives;
   (b) was in the lawful possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, free of any obligation of confidence;
   (c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party not under any obligation of confidence to the Disclosing Party; or
   (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.2 If the Receiving Party is required by law or by any regulator to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall, where legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice and disclose only such part as it is legally required to disclose.

## 4. RETURN OF INFORMATION

4.1 On the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall within ten (10) business days return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except for copies it must retain by law or that are held in routine electronic back-ups, which remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

## 5. DATA PROTECTION

5.1 Each Party shall comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in respect of any personal data forming part of the Confidential Information it receives, and shall notify the other Party of any personal data incident affecting that information without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

## 6. DURATION

6.1 This Agreement shall come into force on the Effective Date and shall continue for two (2) years unless terminated earlier by either Party on thirty (30) days' written notice.

6.2 Each Party's obligations under this Agreement shall survive expiry or termination for three (3) years.

## 7. NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES

7.1 During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after its expiry or termination, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it had contact in connection with the Proposed Transaction.

7.2 Clause 7.1 does not restrict either Party from hiring any person who responds to a general advertisement or recruitment campaign not targeted at the other Party's employees, or who approaches that Party on their own initiative.

## 8. NO REPRESENTATION; NO OBLIGATION; NO LICENCE

8.1 Confidential Information is provided "as is" and neither Party makes any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

8.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to proceed with the Proposed Transaction or restricts either Party from providing services to any other person.

8.3 No licence under any intellectual property right of either Party is granted by this Agreement.

## 9. REMEDIES

9.1 Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a failure to comply with this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief or specific performance in addition to any other remedy available at law.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes FinServe Ref FSC/LGL/MNDA/2026/118 as first issued on 25 September 2026.

10.2 No amendment or waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorised signatories of both Parties.

10.3 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate on written notice.

10.4 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

10.5 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
